title: "Don't invent infrastructure"
description: "You cannot vibe-code a database—yet. Build on best-in-breed OSS that has stood the test of time."

Building distributed systems, infrastructure, and compute primitives is hard—really hard. Prefer platforms that have already paid the complexity tax.

When the problem is large enough, inventing infrastructure can still be the right call.
