---
title: "Practice over preaching"
description: "Trust those who can do over those who talk."
weight: 130
---

There has never been more snake oil in the business of how-to-do-X. Frameworks, playbooks, and LinkedIn sermons multiply faster than working systems.

Trust people and partners who have shipped and operated under real constraints. Preaching without practice is a content strategy, not an engineering one.

In platform work especially, the gap between slide and production is where the money burns. Prefer operators with scars.
