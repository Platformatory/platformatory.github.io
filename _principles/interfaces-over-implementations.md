---
title: "Interfaces over implementations"
description: "Own the contracts. Swap the boxes. Platforms die when every team couples to every vendor’s SDK."
weight: 60
---

Own the contracts your organization speaks, events, APIs, schemas, workflows.

Implementations will change. Vendors will change. If teams couple to SDKs instead of interfaces, you don’t have a platform; you have a pile of integrations waiting to break in the same week.
