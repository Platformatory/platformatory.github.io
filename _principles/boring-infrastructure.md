---
title: "Prefer boring infrastructure"
description: "Novelty belongs in the product. The substrate should be dull, operable, and widely understood."
weight: 50
---

Save invention for the customer-facing surface.

The data plane, the control plane, and the runtime should be boring on purpose: operable at 3am, hireable in the market, and boring enough that your best people work on outcomes instead of babysitting exotic stacks.
