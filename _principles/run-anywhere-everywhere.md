Lock-in is a pricing strategy wearing an architecture costume. Portability across clouds and edges is leverage: the ability to choose, renegotiate, and place workloads where economics and latency win.

Hybrid is not a compromise. It is the default shape of serious estates—regulated data here, burst compute there, edge inference where the device already is.

Design for run-anywhere early enough that migration is a parameter, not a multi-year hostage situation.
