Modern software engineering is mostly the discipline of choosing well.

Ubiquitous, open-protocol interfaces (Kafka, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, Temporal, and their peers) compound. They hire with you, they outlive vendors, and they keep your platform from becoming a museum of one-off decisions.
