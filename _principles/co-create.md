---
title: "Co-create"
description: "Creating alpha requires expertise, not headcount. Build with the best in the business."
weight: 150
---

Alpha comes from craft on the critical path, not from staffing theater. More people around a poorly framed problem usually makes a larger poorly framed problem.

Co-creation puts expert builders beside your domain owners—shared design, shared delivery, shared accountability. Partnership beats body shopping when the work is differentiating.

Hire for leverage. Partner for depth. Do not confuse either with headcount as a proxy for progress.
