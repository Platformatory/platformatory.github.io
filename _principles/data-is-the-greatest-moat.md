---
title: "Data is the greatest moat"
description: "AI that isn't grounded to your business alpha is useless. The new charter for software engineering is to be able to act upon it. Every company is in the intelligence business."
weight: 10
---

Models are abundant. Differentiated data (and the ability to act on it in production) is not.

If your AI cannot touch the alpha of your business, it is a demo. The engineering charter is no longer “ship features”; it is “make the organization able to sense and decide.” Every serious company is in the intelligence business, whether they named it that way or not.
