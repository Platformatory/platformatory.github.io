---
title: "Build and operate"
description: "The best teams build what they operate and operate what they build."
weight: 160
---

Throwing systems over the wall creates two failures: builders who never feel runtime pain, and operators who cannot change the design.

Interleave the loops. The people who ship should feel the pager; the people who operate should have a path to change the code and the platform paths underneath.

Platform teams especially fail when they only provision and never own outcomes. Build-and-operate is how paved paths stay honest.
