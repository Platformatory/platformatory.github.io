---
title: "Measure what compounds"
description: "Vanity metrics flatter. Compounding metrics (lead time, recovery, reuse, and data freshness) tell you if the platform is working."
weight: 90
---

If you only measure usage, you will optimize for noise.

Measure what compounds: lead time to production, mean time to recovery, reuse of platform paths, and freshness of the data that decisions depend on. Those numbers tell you whether the platform is earning its keep.
