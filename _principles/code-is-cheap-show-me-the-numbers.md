---
title: "Code is cheap, show me the numbers"
description: "Application-layer coding costs ~0. Everyone has the same models. Impact must be measurable—code is not the benchmark."
weight: 220
---

The cost of coding—most things in the application layer—is approaching zero. Everyone has access to the same frontier models. Everyone is token-maxxing. Generating repositories is no longer scarce, and coding is no longer a reliable benchmark for technical skill.

In that world, software-as-creation-process has to prove itself in a more tangible sense: latency, cost per unit of work, conversion, reliability, margin, time-to-decision. Numbers that survive contact with production—not demos that survive contact with a slide.

Show the impact. Ship what you can measure. In the face of slop, accountability is the scarce input.
