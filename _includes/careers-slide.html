Careers

We're looking for engineers.

Engineers who are ready to immerse themselves in business problems, willing to go technically deep. A sound sense of data. Everyone at Platformatory is a practitioner. We do not care about degrees, credentials or other pedigree.

Career progression at Platformatory is different. We're building a new-age collective in a post-AI milieu. Growth will be competence based (contrary to years of experience). Many senior team members become partners in a collective sense. This can be financially very rewarding. However, this may not be for everyone: some consider entry-level salaries to be low (this is true).

If you're starting out as a beginner, we highly recommend you enroll into www.dataplusai.academy. This provides a pathway to Platformatory and a rewarding career with us or otherwise.
