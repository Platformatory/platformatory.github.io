  Live accessibility workloads inherit media pain—jitter, failover, session affinity—plus a non-negotiable human outcome.

  Caption pipelines could not trade latency for scale.

  Low-latency streaming infrastructure for real-time captioning: Kafka / Flink on Kubernetes, gRPC service paths, session-scale operability so end-to-end latency stayed inside the product SLA as concurrent sessions grew.
