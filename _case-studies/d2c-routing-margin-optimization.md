the_customer: |
  A Bay Area digital native where every fulfillment choice is a margin choice.

  Carrier mix, node selection, and promise dates were decided on data that was too late or too fragmented.

challenge: |
  Optimization engines inherited stale joins of cost, capacity, and service level.

  Weekly planning windows could not reflect the live network.

work: |
  Data and decision paths for routing and margin: Kafka / Flink into Aerospike-backed online features and Spark / Databricks optimization loops—so margin and promise tradeoffs became continuous operable decisions.

impact:
  - "Fresher inputs into routing and margin optimization loops"
  - "Clearer join of cost, capacity, and service-level signals"
  - "Decision data path operations could run continuously—not only in planning windows"
