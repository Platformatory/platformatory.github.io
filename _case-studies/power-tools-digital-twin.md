  A global power tools manufacturer advancing Industry 4.0 across connected devices and plants.

challenge: |
  Twin programs fail when event models, device identity, and plant connectivity are underspecified—or when every plant invents its own ingest.

  Slide-only narratives do not survive production.

work: |
  Sparring partner and architecture advisory: Kafka / Connect / Flink boundaries for real-time device tracking and lifecycle management, tenancy and contract shapes so the twin stayed an operable platform program—not a perpetual PoC.
