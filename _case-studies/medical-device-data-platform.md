the_customer: |
  A global medical device leader where analytics ambition sat next to regulated product and clinical-adjacent processes.

  Teams wanted lakehouse speed without improvising compliance at the edges.

challenge: |
  Scientists and product teams needed Databricks-shaped iteration; security and quality needed lineage and controlled movement.

  Copy-everything-to-the-lake failed both speed and audit.

work: |
  Data platform and streaming architecture: Kafka / Flink / Confluent as the movement spine, Databricks / Spark for lakehouse analytics, with contracts so regulated data could travel once and analytics teams could build without inventing a second shadow estate.

impact:
  - "Streaming + lakehouse architecture aligned to regulated healthcare constraints"
  - "Clearer contracts for data movement into analytics environments"
  - "Platform posture reusable across device and commercial analytics programs"
