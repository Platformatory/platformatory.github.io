the_customer: |
  A global industrial conglomerate whose business units had independently grown analytics estates across clouds.

  Corporate wanted modernization without requiring every BU to abandon its cloud.

challenge: |
  N warehouses, N ETL religions, N security models.

  A one-mega-platform mandate would stall; unmanaged multi-cloud was already chaos.

work: |
  Modernization spine across clouds: Kafka / Flink / Confluent for movement where freshness mattered, Databricks / Spark for lakehouse analytics, Druid and ClickHouse for interactive serving, plus contracts and migration sequencing so BUs could adopt shared primitives without a forced single-cloud rewrite.

impact:
  - "Multi-cloud lakehouse and streaming boundaries BUs could adopt"
  - "Clearer modernization path without a forced single-cloud rewrite"
  - "Shared analytics primitives that reduced per-BU reinvention"
