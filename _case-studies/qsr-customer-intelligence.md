the_customer: |
  A global quick-service restaurant brand portfolio. Loyalty and offers have to feel local to each brand’s guests, and still compound as shared customer intelligence across the estate.

challenge: |
  Drive loyalty through real-time offers across in-store experience, app, and campaigns—without collapsing brand and CMO autonomy into one marketing monoculture.

  Enriched insight had to travel as shared products and features, not as a mandate that every brand surrender its voice.

work: |
  Built a unified data hub on Databricks, Confluent, and Kafka Connect. Stood up a feature store for high-cardinality user attributes. Published golden data products—Customer 360, Product, and Campaign Performance—and propensity modelling across the brand / product / regional portfolio.

  Ingested clickstream across properties and Google Analytics. Reverse ETL into Braze and other activation systems so offers could fire in-store, in-app, and in campaigns from the same hub.

impact:
  - "Networked customer intelligence across brands with brand & CMO autonomy preserved"
  - "Real-time loyalty offers on in-store, app, and campaign surfaces"
  - "Golden products (Customer 360, Product, Campaign Performance) + high-cardinality feature store"
  - "Propensity across brand / product / region; Reverse ETL to Braze and activation partners"
